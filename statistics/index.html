Statistics 101

Someone counted. Someone didn't get counted. Until you can name both, you don't have a statistic.

Self-paced No login No server First-year / gen-ed

This sits next to Analytical Reasoning. That course treats a number in ordinary talk. This one stays with a table. Same refusal, different object.

Small tables. Labeled EXAMPLES. One public weather summary. No software. About four to six hours. Course samples

The job

  1. Rewrite a number so the group and the denominator are in the sentence.
  2. Choose a center, and say in one sentence what it is hiding.
  3. Name the scale, window, or missing piece a chart is using as an argument.
  4. Tell a sample from a population, and name a group the sample did not reach.
  5. Restate a probability as a long-run frequency under a named process.
  6. Name an association, a confounder, and a sentence that does not leap.
  7. Translate “significant” without treating it as important, proven, or a trick.
  8. Make a claim a small table can actually buy, and name a limit.
