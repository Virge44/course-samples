# Chemistry 101 — draft course sample

A playable eight-unit college 101 for first-year / gen-ed students. Instructional-design sample by Virgil Renfroe. **He did not teach chemistry as a content expert.** Lecturer courses were Critical Writing, Humanities, and Analytical Reasoning. As Director of Online Learning he designed an online course called Scientific Development & Social Change — that is design/ops, not a chemistry appointment. Do not invent labs, credentials, or “I taught this at A&T.”

**Not a live institutional course. Not official NC A&T curriculum.** Do not apply anywhere. Do not treat this as credit, placement, or a current offering. No student data. No invented school partners. Subject facts are conservative.

**Open the course:** keep this folder together and double-click `index.html` in Chrome, Safari, Firefox, or Edge. Do not rely on a Google Drive (or similar) in-browser preview — the practice activities will not run. No server, no login, relative links only.

## Honesty

Frame: a first-year gen-ed sample showing he can structure a course (objectives, lesson, practice with feedback, homework, sequence). It is not a chemistry appointment and not a lab manual.

The author is an educator and online-learning lead who designs learning and ships working prototypes by directing AI coding agents. He is not a software engineer.

## Safety

No explosives, no drug synthesis, no weaponization, no “how to make X,” no lab recipes for hazardous chemicals, no chlorine gas, no extraction how-tos. Thought experiments and reading labels only. Household products appear as labels and typical published values — no mixing instructions.
