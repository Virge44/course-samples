# Module 1 — times and snags

See `facilitator-note.md` for what not to claim.

About 25 minutes: lesson (~10), practice in `module-1.html` (~10), homework prompt (~5). Mental-model check, two failure modes, red lines, verify habit. CRISP rebuild is Module 3. Do not send people to a separate pack.

They should leave able to explain the tool in plain language, name confident wrongness and wrong level, and keep a short list of what never goes in the box.

Homework goes in an LMS journal or a shared doc. A class they actually teach, not how they feel about AI. If a cohort exists, skim for the two failure modes and one red-line mention. You do not need to grade them.

Snags: they want a tool demo (tools are Module 3). They collapse everything into cheating (Module 2). They skip verify because the output looked done. They paste a real student name or IEP line while they practice — say that out loud once if you debrief. Someone asks who wrote the seventh-grade case: I have not taught that room.

Optional debrief, only if a cohort exists: one confident-wrongness example, one red-line check. Stop.
