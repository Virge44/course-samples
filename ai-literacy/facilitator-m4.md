# Module 4 — times and snags

See `facilitator-note.md` for what not to claim.

About 25 minutes: lesson (~8), activity-card builder + stress test in `module-4.html` (~12), homework (~5). They leave with a card.

Pilot the card in one class this term, or write why they will not. “Why not” is a complete answer. Do not pressure a public pilot.

Snags: AI writes the thing students were supposed to learn. No offline path. Detectors as the integrity plan. No stop rule. A showcase lesson they will never teach.

Do not present the demo card as a required district activity. Do not invent a partner school. Activity cards in both lanes are for the audience. I have not held a K–12 teaching job.

Optional debrief: one card read aloud. What is the AI not allowed to do? How does a student without a device still meet the goal? Stop.
