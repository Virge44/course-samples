# Facilitator note — Module 4: Classroom Activities That Hold

For a PD lead running this async. Colleague voice.

**Author frame (if asked).** Higher-ed teaching and faculty development. Has not held a K–12 teaching job. Activity cards in both lanes are for the audience.

## Time

About 25 minutes: lesson (~8), activity-card builder + stress test (~12), homework (~5). Practice is in `module-4.html`. They leave with a card, not a demo.

## Objectives

1. Assign AI a narrow role that serves a learning goal (or name when AI stays out).
2. Build an activity card: timing, student steps, disclosure, success evidence, stop rule.
3. Stress-test the card for equity and integrity (offline path; human performance still graded).

## Homework

Pilot the card in one class this term, *or* write why they will not. “Why not” is a complete answer. Do not pressure a public pilot.

## Common snags

- AI writes the thing students were supposed to learn. Narrow the role or drop AI.
- No offline path. Mixed devices are normal. The card must still work on paper.
- Detectors as the integrity plan. Redesign the evidence instead.
- Skipping the stop rule. If they cannot name when AI leaves the room, the card is not ready.
- Building a showcase lesson they will never teach. Push toward one real class this term.

## What not to claim

Do not present the demo card as a required district activity. Do not invent a partner school.

## Optional 15-minute debrief

One card read aloud. Room asks: What is the AI *not* allowed to do? How does a student without a device still meet the goal? Stop.
