# Module 5 — times and snags

See `facilitator-note.md` for what not to claim.

About 20 minutes: lesson (~8), six-line note + two-week sketch in `module-5.html` (~7), homework (~5). One class. Two weeks.

They send the six-line note to one real person, or paste it into a journal. Do not invent a recipient. Do not require a public post.

Snags: waiting for a task force. Making a new rule required on day one. Writing the note as a manifesto. Asking you for a partner school or a canned district rollout.

Do not present this course as adopted policy. Do not name fictional campuses. Do not tell them to require AI.

Optional debrief: two people read their notes. Class named, allowed/not, how they will check, when they report back. Stop.
