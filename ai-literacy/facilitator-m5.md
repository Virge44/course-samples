# Facilitator note — Module 5: Take It Back

For a PD lead running this async. Colleague voice.

**Author frame (if asked).** Educator who has run faculty PD and online programs. Not a change-management consultant. No invented school partners in this module.

## Time

About 20 minutes: lesson (~8), six-line note + two-week sketch (~7), homework (~5). Practice is in `module-5.html`. The point is a tired educator implementing this week without a committee.

## Objectives

1. Tell a colleague, chair, or PD lead what they are trying in plain language (optional vs required).
2. Name what they will tell students this week.
3. Sketch a two-week pilot they can actually run, or name why they will wait.

## Homework

Send the six-line “what I’m trying” note to one real person — or paste it into an LMS journal if they have no colleague to send it to. Do not invent a recipient. Do not require a public post.

## Common snags

- Waiting for a task force. This module is for one class, two weeks.
- Making a new rule required on day one. Optional-with-disclosure is enough for a first pilot.
- Writing the note as a manifesto. Six lines. A colleague should be able to read it between classes.
- Asking the facilitator for a partner school or a canned “district rollout.” There isn’t one.

## What not to claim

Do not present this course as adopted policy. Do not name fictional campuses. Do not tell them to require AI.

## Optional 10-minute debrief

Two people read their notes. Check: class named, allowed/not, how they will check, when they report back. Stop.
