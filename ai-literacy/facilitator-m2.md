# Module 2 — times and snags

See `facilitator-note.md` for what not to claim.

About 25 minutes: lesson (~10), practice in `module-2.html` (~10), homework (~5). Place three uses, rewrite one policy sentence, one borderline case. Conference first.

They rewrite their own syllabus or classroom AI line. Allowed / disclose / not allowed / talk-first. Not legal polish. Local policy still governs.

Snags: they want a detector policy (detectors are weak evidence alone). “Any AI touch is cheating” — hold the spectrum. They rewrite into longer fog. They treat a fabricated quote as a cheating case (that is accuracy in materials they assigned). They jump to an office referral on the borderline case.

Optional debrief: one person reads their rewrite. The room checks allowed, disclose, not allowed, privacy, talk-first. Stop.
