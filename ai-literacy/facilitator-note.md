# Facilitator note — AI Literacy for Educators

One page. What not to claim. For a PD lead running the async.

I am Virgil Renfroe. Lecturer at NC A&T: Critical Writing, large-enrollment Analytical Reasoning, Humanities. Director of Online Learning, 2008–2012. MFA, Creative Writing, UNCG. I have not held a K–12 teaching job. I am not a software engineer. This is not a course I taught at A&T. The K–12 lanes are for the people in the room.

## Do not say

- Do not introduce me as a K–12 teacher.
- Do not introduce me as a software engineer, or as someone who “ships prototypes by directing AI coding agents.”
- Do not call this a live institutional course, a submitted product, or official NC A&T curriculum.
- Do not invent a partner school, a district rollout, or a campus policy I wrote.
- Do not sell a vendor. Any school-approved tool. CRISP is a habit, not a brand.
- Do not treat detectors as the integrity plan.
- Do not require student AI use as the price of finishing the PD.
- Do not use hype (“unlock,” “rapidly evolving landscape”) if you add a welcome note.

## What this is

Five modules, about two hours. Literacy, integrity, tools, a classroom activity, a note for Monday. Practice lives in the HTML. Homework happens in their class. No student records in this file. Nothing uploads.

If someone asks who wrote the seventh-grade case: I did, for the audience. Point at the bio. Do not imply I taught that room.

## Time

1 Literacy ~25 · 2 Integrity ~25 · 3 Tools ~20 · 4 Activities ~25 · 5 A note for Monday ~20.
