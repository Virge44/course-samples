AI Literacy for Educators

This is two hours of async PD for people who already teach. You leave with a rule you can use Monday. I’m not selling a tool, and I haven’t been K–12 faculty.

Self-paced No login No server K–12 and higher ed

Open this page in Chrome, Safari, or Firefox. Practice runs here. Nothing is uploaded. Progress stays in this tab.

I am Virgil Renfroe. I taught and designed in higher ed — Lecturer at NC A&T in Critical Writing, large-enrollment Analytical Reasoning, and Humanities, and Director of Online Learning from 2008 to 2012. MFA, Creative Writing, UNCG. I have not held a K–12 teaching job. The K–12 lanes in these modules are for you, not a claim about my classroom. This is not a course I taught at A&T.

Sequence

ModuleTimeYou leave able to
1. What GenAI Actually Does
Literacy		 ~25 min Explain the tool in plain language; name two failure modes; keep red lines and a verify habit.
2. Integrity Without Panic
Ethics		 ~25 min Place uses on a spectrum; separate authorship from privacy, bias, and labor; rewrite one policy line.
3. Tools That Transfer
Tools		 ~20 min Rebuild a weak prompt with CRISP; run a verify loop; say when a template beats a model.
4. Classroom Activities That Hold
Practice		 ~25 min Build one activity card with a stop rule and an equity check.
5. Take It Back
Responsible adoption		 ~20 min Write a six-line note a colleague can read, and sketch a two-week pilot — or say why you will wait.

What you will do

Each module is a short lesson, some practice in the page, and one homework prompt you can paste into a journal. About two hours if you do the practice. The homework is in your class, not in this file.

Use the tool your school already approved. I am not picking a vendor.
