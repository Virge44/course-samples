# AI Literacy for Educators — draft course

A playable five-module async PD course for faculty and K–12 educators. Draft sample by Virgil Renfroe. Not a live institutional course. Not submitted. No student data.

**Open the course:** download this folder and double-click `index.html` in Chrome, Safari, or Firefox. Do not rely on a Google Drive (or similar) in-browser preview — the practice activities will not run. No server, no login, relative links only.

## Honesty

This is a work sample for an AI Literacy Course Developer role. Practice lives inside each module. An earlier three-module interactive pack existed as a practice-layer sample; this folder is the course. Do not treat this as submitted to AI for Education.

The author is an educator and online-learning lead who designs learning and ships working prototypes by directing AI coding agents. He is not a software engineer. He has not held a K–12 teaching job; K–12 lanes are for the audience.
