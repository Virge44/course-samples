# AI Literacy for Educators — draft course

Two hours of async PD for people who already teach. Five modules. Draft sample by Virgil Renfroe. Not a live institutional course. Not submitted. No student data. Not a course I taught at A&T.

Open `index.html` in Chrome, Safari, or Firefox. Do not rely on a Drive preview — the practice will not run. No server, no login.

I am Virgil Renfroe. Lecturer at NC A&T: Critical Writing, large-enrollment Analytical Reasoning, Humanities. Director of Online Learning, 2008–2012. MFA, Creative Writing, UNCG. I have not held a K–12 teaching job. The K–12 lanes are for the audience.
