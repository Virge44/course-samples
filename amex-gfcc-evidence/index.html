TPS evidence checklist — teaching frame

What an instructional designer would gather. Not confidential American Express policy. Not clauses of the Training Global Standard. I did not invent that standard’s text.

  • The version that was live on the as-of date, not only the file on the author’s laptop today.
  • SME sign-off on that version (BSA policy / GFCC policy), with date and scope.
  • Who was in scope, who was assigned, who completed, who is overdue, who is not in scope.
  • Attestation language the learner actually saw.
  • A map from each beat to a public requirement (not an invented internal clause).
  • A trail complete enough that Internal Audit can test whether TPS followed its own evidence practice, and an examiner can see assignment, live content, and in-scope completions without a rebuilt course.
