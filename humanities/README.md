# Humanities 101 — draft course sample

A playable eight-unit first-year course: look hard at a made thing, say what it is doing, say why that matters to a living person. Draft sample by Virgil Renfroe. **Not a live institutional course. Not official NC A&T curriculum.** Do not apply it anywhere as a catalog offering. Do not treat teaching texts or sample paragraphs as student records.

Written in the grain of Humanities I & II as the author taught them as Lecturer, University Studies, NC A&T (2004–2016). It is not those courses. It is not currently taught at A&T.

**Open the course:** keep this folder together. Double-click `index.html` in Chrome, Safari, Firefox, or Edge. Do not rely on a Google Drive (or similar) in-browser preview — the practice activities will not run. No server, no login, relative links only. `styles.css` must sit next to the HTML files.

## Texts (copyright-safe)

Public-domain excerpts kept short, plus invented teaching texts labeled as such:

- Teaching retelling of the Elpenor beat, *Odyssey* 11 (not a published translation)
- Frederick Douglass, *Narrative of the Life* (1845), three sentences on literacy
- Paul Laurence Dunbar, opening of “We Wear the Mask” (1896)
- Olaudah Equiano, *The Interesting Narrative* (1789), a short look at the ship
- *Receipt*, an original 12-line teaching poem written for this sample

No copyrighted criticism. No poems first published after 1928. Dickinson after 1929 is not used.
