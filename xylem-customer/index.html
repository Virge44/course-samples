Check the network before the rain, not after the overflow.

Xylem is a water-technology company. Their customers include municipal drinking water and wastewater utilities. They publish a smart-sewer case: a utility cut combined-sewer overflows (CSO) by 80%. They also train customers (HYPACK hydrographic software — they list a virtual side-scan course). This page is not that case, not that course, and not a Xylem product.

I storyboarded this clip, wrote the script, and directed it with AI video tools so a hiring reader can watch one teaching beat. I have not used Camtasia, Storyline, Rise, or Adobe CC as production tools. I can learn house tools. I do not have water-utility tenure.

The clip

About 29 seconds. Captions on. One beat: look at this year’s network view before the rain, not after the overflow. Public-safe. No wiring. No bypass. No real plant.

A motion graphic that teaches

Same rain. Two endings. Combined sewers share a pipe for wastewater and stormwater. If nobody checks the network view, the pipe spills. If someone checks before the rain, the overflow is the thing you are trying not to do.

Three stops a customer can actually do

The gap I started with: a utility person still works off last year’s printout, then calls after the overflow. The learning is not a longer course. It is three stops they can do this week, and a page they can keep.

Implement Before rain season, confirm you are on this year’s network view. The current sheet is with the assigned person. Last year’s printout is not a view.
Operate When rain is in the forecast, check the view first. Not after the overflow. Not after the street is already the lesson.
Maintain If the view and the street do not match, stop. Do not improvise a bypass. Call the assigned person. Say what the view showed and what you did not do.

Job aid they can keep

SAMPLE job aid. Smart-sewer / CSO teaching object. Not a Xylem product. Not an IFU.

  1. Implement — this year’s network view, current sheet, assigned person. If any one is missing, you do not have a view.
  2. Operate — rain in the forecast: check the view before the event.
  3. Maintain — view and street disagree: stop. No bypass. Escalate. Say the view, the street, that you did not bypass.
