Check the network before the rain, not after the overflow.

Combined sewers share one pipe for wastewater and stormwater. A person still working from last year’s printout will call after the overflow. I built a short clip, a motion graphic, three stops they can do this week, and a page they can keep.

The clip

Twenty-nine seconds, captions on. Look at this year’s network view before the rain. Public-safe: no wiring, no bypass, no plant data.

Same rain, two endings

If nobody checks the view, the shared pipe spills. If someone checks before the rain, the overflow is what you are trying not to do.

Storyboard

What I would hand an editor. Three shots. The VO is the script.

Three stops a customer can do this week

Implement Before rain season, confirm you are on this year’s network view. The current sheet is with the assigned person. Last year’s printout is not a view.
Operate When rain is in the forecast, check the view first. Not after the overflow. Not after the street is already the lesson.
Maintain If the view and the street do not match, stop. Do not improvise a bypass. Call the assigned person. Say what the view showed and what you did not do.

Job aid they can keep

  1. Implement — this year’s network view, current sheet, assigned person. If any one is missing, you do not have a view.
  2. Operate — rain in the forecast: check the view before the event.
  3. Maintain — view and street disagree: stop. No bypass. Escalate. Say the view, the street, that you did not bypass.
