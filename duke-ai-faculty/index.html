Three beats · literacy, then integrity, then a next step

Beat 1 · about 12 minutes · literacy

Student work stays out of public tools.

The rule you can use this week: do not paste student writing, names, grades, unpublished exams, or research that is not yours into a public tool. If you would not pin it on a board outside your office, it does not go in the box.

This is not a tools tour. Fluency is not the point. The point is what does not leave the room.

Beat 2 · about 16 minutes · integrity

A syllabus line that holds.

One line students can actually follow. Not “don’t cheat.” Not a ban you will not keep. Allowed, disclose, or out of bounds — in a sentence you would read aloud on day one.

Beat 3 · about 12 minutes · a next step

This session, or book 20 minutes.

This session is enough if you leave with the rule and a syllabus line you would actually post. Book 20 minutes if the case is messy: a student file, unpublished exam questions, a department that disagrees, research data, a FERPA edge. I translate. I do not pretend the 45 minutes covers the edge.
