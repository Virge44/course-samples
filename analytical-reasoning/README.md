# Analytical Reasoning 101 — draft course sample

A playable eight-unit college 101 for first-year / gen-ed students. Draft sample by Virgil Renfroe. Not a live institutional course. Not official NC A&T curriculum. Do not apply anywhere. No student data. No invented school partners.

**Open the course:** keep this folder together and double-click `index.html` in Chrome, Safari, or Firefox. Do not rely on a Google Drive (or similar) in-browser preview — the practice activities will not run. No server, no login, relative links only.

## Honesty

Written in the grain of the large-enrollment Analytical Reasoning course Virgil Renfroe taught in University Studies at NC A&T (often 300–600 students a semester, many underprepared), 2004–2016. This folder is a compressed sample (~4–6 hours if students do the practices), not a credit course and not a current offering.

The author is an educator and online-learning lead who designs learning and ships working prototypes by directing AI coding agents. He is not a software engineer. He also did GRE/LSAT logic tutoring; that muscle is in the units. This is still a gen-ed 101, not test prep.
