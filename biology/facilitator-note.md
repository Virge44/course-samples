# Facilitator note — Biology 101

Draft sample. Not official NC A&T curriculum. Do not apply.

## What not to claim

He did not teach biology as a content expert. Lecturer courses were Critical Writing, Humanities, and Analytical Reasoning (University Studies, NC A&T, 2004–2016). As Director of Online Learning (2008–2012) he designed an online course called *Scientific Development & Social Change* — design/ops, not a biology appointment. Do not invent labs, credentials, or “I taught this at A&T.” Do not introduce him as a biologist or a lab instructor. Subject facts are conservative.

## How to run it

Keep the folder together. Open `index.html` in a desktop browser. Practices are in-page JavaScript. Collect homework elsewhere.

Eight units, in order. About four to six hours. Conceptual 101, not a lab. If you stretch it, add *your* readings. Do not add lab protocols to these files.

## Safety — do not assign as a lab

- No pathogen work. No culturing, plating, incubating, or “grow mold on purpose.”
- No genome-editing how-to. No CRISPR steps. No synthesis, reverse genetics, or enhancement.
- No medical advice. Pulse-after-stairs is an observation of a trade, not a fitness test.
- Bread mold, if used: look at mold that already appeared, through a closed bag if possible. Do not sniff. Students who should not handle mold use the bird-at-feeder look or the teaching figure in Unit 8.
- If a student writes a protocol, send it back.

## What to collect

| Unit | Turn-in | Skim for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Rewrite of a just-so story | Parts, interaction, a consequence that could be wrong |
| 2 | One cell job as a system | Membrane or traffic. A job that depends on another. |
| 3 | Roommate paragraph: DNA and proteins | What the molecule *does*. Flag CRISPR. |
| 4 | Food → work chain | Transformation, not “energy boost.” |
| 5 | Variation / selection / time | All three. Flag “the strongest survive.” |
| 6 | A real place they can see | Constraints. Flag “balance of nature.” |
| 7 | One bodily trade | Variable in range. Not a diagnosis. |
| 8 | Evidence writeup, 250–400 words | Claim, observation, what would count against it. No protocol. |

## Common snags

Unit 1 as a warmup and purpose-stories anyway — send them back. Unit 3 becoming a genome-editing essay — refuse. Unit 7 as WebMD — conceptual trades only. Unit 8 as a kitchen lab — observation of what is already there.
