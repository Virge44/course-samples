# Biology 101 — draft course sample

A playable eight-unit first-year gen-ed course: name a part, say what it does to another part, say what would come out different. Draft instructional-design sample by Virgil Renfroe. **Not a live institutional course. Not official NC A&T curriculum.** Do not apply it anywhere as a catalog offering. Do not treat teaching figures as student records.

**Honesty lock.** He did **not** teach biology as a content expert. Lecturer courses were Critical Writing, Humanities, and Analytical Reasoning (University Studies, NC A&T, 2004–2016). As Director of Online Learning (2008–2012) he designed an online course called *Scientific Development & Social Change* — that is design/ops, not a biology appointment. Do not invent labs, credentials, or “I taught this at A&T.” Frame: a first-year gen-ed sample showing he can structure a course. Subject facts conservative.

**Open the course:** keep this folder together. Double-click `index.html` in Chrome, Safari, Firefox, or Edge. Do not rely on a Google Drive (or similar) in-browser preview — the practice activities will not run. No server, no login, relative links only. `styles.css` must sit next to the HTML files.

## Safety

Conceptual only. No pathogen work. No enhancement, synthesis, or reverse genetics. No lab protocols for microbes. No CRISPR / genome-editing how-to. No medical advice. Unit 8 is a household look or a teaching figure — not a culture, not a diagnosis.
