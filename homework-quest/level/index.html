The case

I wrote a tiny gold set for a child who can already read short decodable lines and is stretching into two-clause sentences. That child is a teaching case, not a real student.

The gold key for the set is instructional. You will mark four example lines the way that child would meet them tonight. Then I will tell you the band the tool landed, the one task I would do today, and whether that matched the gold key.

Alone = independent on that line. A little help = instructional. Not tonight = too hard for tonight.
