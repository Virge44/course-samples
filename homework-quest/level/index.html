What can they read tonight?

I designed this so a grown-up can check, in two minutes, what a child can read out loud tonight. Not a test score. Not a grade. The game stays the adventure.

Four example lines. Not a published test.

How this works

I already know how a child at this spot would do on these four lines. That child is a teaching case, not a real student. They can read a short line like “The fox ran to the hill.” They are stretching into longer sentences. The science sentence is too much for tonight.

You mark the four lines the way that child would meet them. Then I show whether your marks match what I already knew, and one sentence to read — or not — today.
