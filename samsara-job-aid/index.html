Card 1 · SAMPLE · new driver

First week. What to check before the first run.

Do not start the run to “figure it out on the road.” Three checks. If any one is missing, stop and ask.

  1. Today’s assignment is in your hand — who, where, by when. Not yesterday’s list.
  2. The vehicle you were given is the vehicle on that assignment.
  3. You have a working way to call in (the number on the current sheet, not a number someone texted last year).

If any check fails: do not start the run. Ask the dispatcher (SAMPLE: M. Chen). Not a product tour. Not a telematics IFU.

Card 2 · SAMPLE · when a device is offline

When a device is offline: three things to try, then escalate.

Do not keep guessing. Do not improvise a bypass. Try these three. Then hand it off.

  1. Confirm you are looking at the vehicle you were assigned — the ID on the sheet, not the one next to it.
  2. The obvious: is it powered, is the cable seated, did it just leave a stretch with no signal.
  3. Write the time and what you already tried, in one line.

Then escalate with that line. SAMPLE contact: yard desk, R. Patel. Not a repair. Not a product model.

Card 3 · SAMPLE · coaching a stuck teammate

Coaching a stuck teammate: one question, one next step.

They do not need a deck. They need one question, then one thing to do.

  • One question: “What did you already try?” Wait for the answer. Do not recap the week.
  • One next step: name the card that matches (first run, or offline), or escalate with them. Sit with the card. Do not send forty slides.
