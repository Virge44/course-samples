## How to run it

Keep the folder together. Point students at `index.html` in a desktop browser, not a Drive preview. Practices are in-page JavaScript. Collect homework in your LMS journal, a shared folder, or on paper. This file does not store it.

Eight units, in order. Each sitting: a lesson, a practice with feedback, one turn-in (150–400 words or a revision of their own paragraph). About four to six hours if they do the practices. Compressed 101, not a fifteen-week research sequence. If you stretch it across a term, use the units as weekly spines and add *your* readings. Do not add this sample’s invented paragraphs to a bibliography.

## What to collect

| Unit | Turn-in | What to skim for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Reader / purpose / stakes sentence + one paragraph | Named reader with a belief; a verb you can watch; a local cost. Flag “people,” “society,” “it matters.” |
| 2 | Four sentence-moves + two sentences of analysis | Moves named; analysis that does not recap. |
| 3 | Thesis + honest objection + what would change | Thesis a classmate could refuse; objection that is not a dummy. |
| 4 | Claim, two reasons, one evidence, one gap | Reasons that fail differently; evidence labeled by kind; no “I feel,” no “studies show” with no study. |
| 5 | Paragraph rewrite, one job | A verb for the job; split if it was a pile. |
| 6 | Bad full draft, 300–400 words | Complete object: claim, two reasons, honest evidence, an ending that is not “important.” Ugly is allowed. |
| 7 | Revision memo | Claim changed or defended after a real objection; a cut named by failed job; edits saved for last. |
| 8 | Short argument + 3–5 sentences on what changed | Source used as a voice, not a tour; invented lines still labeled invented; no fake journal. |

Grade the claim a stranger could test, not fluency-as-virtue.

## Common snags

- Skipping Unit 1 and writing to “the teacher.” Send them back.
- Treating Unit 6 as optional. Without a full messy draft, Unit 7 becomes proofreading.
- Starting Unit 7 at comma splices. The notecard pass is the assignment.
- Quoting a tool-generated tour of sources in Unit 8 and calling it research.
- Inventing campus counts or a journal to look complete. Named holes are honest.
- Students asking if this *is* the old A&T course. Say: written in that grain; not official; not current.

## What not to claim

Do not introduce me as a software engineer. Do not call this official NC A&T curriculum or a live A&T section. Do not invent school partners or student data. Do not treat the invented Ortiz line, the visitor-lot tally, or the group-grade survey as real sources. They are practice objects.

## Optional live 20 minutes

Only if a cohort exists. Three volunteers read their Unit 1 sentence. Class says whether a stranger could disagree. One Unit 3 dead thesis rewritten on the board. Stop. Do not turn it into a grammar workshop.

## Accessibility

Pages are keyboardable. Practices give feedback in-page, not only as color. If you need a no-JavaScript path, the homework prompts still work as writing assignments; the in-page practice will not.
