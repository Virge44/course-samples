# Facilitator note — Physics 101

Draft sample. Not official NC A&T curriculum. Do not apply.

## What not to claim

He did not teach physics as a content expert. Lecturer courses were Critical Writing, Humanities, and Analytical Reasoning (University Studies, NC A&T, 2004–2016). As Director of Online Learning (2008–2012) he designed an online course called *Scientific Development & Social Change* — design/ops, not a physics appointment. Do not invent labs, credentials, or “I taught this at A&T.” Do not introduce him as a physicist or a software engineer. Subject facts are conservative textbook 101.

## How to run it

Keep the folder together. Open `index.html` in a desktop browser, not a Drive preview. Practices are in-page JavaScript. Nothing is uploaded. Collect homework in your LMS, a shared folder, or on paper.

Eight units, in order. About four to six hours if they do the practice. Compressed conceptual 101 — not calculus, not a lab. If you stretch it, use the units as weekly spines and add *your* readings or *your* supervised lab. Do not add “how to build,” weapons, or explosives.

Hand numbers already in the pages, labeled as approximations: ~10 N of weight per kilogram; free-fall speedup ~10 m/s each second if air is ignored; sound in room air ~340 m/s; typical U.S. outlet ~120 V. Do not invent a class average and paste it in.

## What to collect

| Unit | Turn-in | Skim for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Two rewritten claims + one paragraph | Quantity, unit, revision test |
| 2 | A real trip in numbers + one graph sentence | Speed with a unit. Slope used. |
| 3 | One everyday scene, forces named | Pair / keep-doing / change-of-motion. Not “gravity is a theory.” |
| 4 | Energy bookkeeping of one household event | Where it went. Flag “energy disappeared.” |
| 5 | A safe collision in numbers | p = mv once. No crash theater. |
| 6 | One sound + one v = fλ | Jobs, not a vocab dump |
| 7 | Lamp + switch as a circuit | Complete path. They did not open an outlet. |
| 8 | Investigation, 250–400 words | One real observable. No invented table. |

## Safety

Thought labs and safe household observation only. No opening outlets. No climbing for a drop. No heating a sealed container. No standing in a street. If you add a live session, you own the briefing.

## Common snags

Unit 1 written as slogans anyway — send them back. Unit 2 as a formula dump — they need to *read a graph*. Unit 7 students opening an outlet — stop. Unit 8 inventing a tidy table they did not measure — one real look beats a fake lab.

If someone asks whether he taught physics at A&T: no.
