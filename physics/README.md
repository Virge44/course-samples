# Physics 101 — draft course sample

A playable eight-unit first-year gen-ed course: conceptual physics with numbers a student can do by hand. A physical claim names a quantity, a unit, and what would change your mind. Draft sample by Virgil Renfroe. **Not a live institutional course. Not official NC A&T curriculum.** Do not apply it anywhere as a catalog offering. Do not treat teaching sentences as student records or as data from a lab that ran.

**Honesty lock.** He did not teach physics as a content expert. Lecturer courses were Critical Writing, Humanities, and Analytical Reasoning. As Director of Online Learning he designed an online course called *Scientific Development & Social Change* — that is design/ops, not a physics appointment. Do not invent labs, credentials, or “I taught this at A&T.” Frame: first-year gen-ed sample showing he can structure a course. Subject facts conservative. No apply.

**Open the course:** keep this folder together. Double-click `index.html` in Chrome, Safari, Firefox, or Edge. Do not rely on a Google Drive (or similar) in-browser preview — the practice activities will not run. No server, no login, relative links only. `styles.css` must sit next to the HTML files.

## Honesty
