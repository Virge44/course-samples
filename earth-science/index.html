Who this is for

First-year and gen-ed college students, mixed preparation. Adult tone. You do not need a rock hammer or a forecast app. You need a habit: say what you claim about the Earth, name the check, and admit what the record cannot show.

This is not a geology major. It is not a climate sermon and not a conspiracy hunt. It is a compressed 101 about evidence: deep time, rocks, plates, water, weather, climate as a measured argument, resources, and one real place you can point to.

What the course asks you to do

Each unit has three measurable objectives, a lesson (read it; it is the work), clickable practice with feedback, and one homework you could turn in. If you do the practices, plan on about four to six hours for the whole sample. A live semester would slow this down, add a textbook, and give you more drafts. This folder is the spine, not fifteen weeks of coverage.

An Earth claim is a sentence about the planet that a classmate could check. A vibe is not a claim. An incomplete record is not a failure of nerve. It is the working condition of the science.

Sequence

UnitTimeYou leave able to
1. What an Earth claim is
Deep time, incomplete record		 ~35 min Split a vibe from a checkable Earth sentence, and name a hole in the record.
2. Minerals and rocks as evidence
Not decoration		 ~40 min Tell mineral from rock, and match a rock to a formation story.
3. Plate tectonics
The ground is not finished		 ~40 min Name three boundary types and why mountains and quakes are process, not scenery.
4. Water
Rivers, groundwater, the cheap-looking miracle		 ~40 min Trace a drop, and distinguish river work from “water is important.”
5. Atmosphere and weather
Not a forecast app		 ~35 min Separate weather from climate, and say what a weather claim needs.
6. Climate as a measured argument
Evidence, not a rant		 ~40 min Treat climate as measurements and proxies, not a mood and not a sermon.
7. Earth resources and waste
Where stuff comes from		 ~35 min Trace one object to a source, and name where waste goes as matter.
8. A local-place writeup
They pick a real hill, river, or weather week		 ~40 min Write three checkable Earth claims about a place you can point to.

How work is judged (if you have a section)

If an instructor used this sample in a live class, the standard would be public, not clever. Did you name an Earth claim? Did you give a check someone else could run? Did you admit a gap in the record? Fluency is not the grade. The checkable step is the grade.

Homework lives in your own words. Paste the prompt into a journal, a discussion board, or a printed page. This file does not collect it. There is no invented field trip and no partner school. Unit 8 asks you to pick a real hill, river, or weather week you can actually point to.
