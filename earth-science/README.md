# Earth Science 101 — draft course sample

A playable eight-unit college 101 for first-year / gen-ed students. Draft sample by Virgil Renfroe. **Not a live institutional course. Not official NC A&T curriculum.** Do not apply anywhere. No student data. No invented school partners. No invented labs or field trips.

**Open the course:** keep this folder together and double-click `index.html` in Chrome, Safari, or Firefox. Do not rely on a Google Drive (or similar) in-browser preview — the practice activities will not run. No server, no login, relative links only.

## Honesty

This is a first-year gen-ed **instructional-design sample** showing he can structure a course. He did **not** teach earth science as a content expert. Lecturer courses were Critical Writing, Humanities, and Analytical Reasoning (University Studies, NC A&T, 2004–2016). As Director of Online Learning (2008–2012) he designed an online course called *Scientific Development & Social Change* — that is design/ops, not an earth-science appointment. Do not invent labs, field trips, credentials, or “I taught this at A&T.”

Subject facts in the units are conservative textbook 101. Climate is treated as a measured argument (evidence habits), not a rant and not a conspiracy. Unit 8 asks the student to pick a real hill, river, or weather week they can point to. No partner campus. No apply.

The author is an educator and online-learning lead who designs learning and ships working prototypes by directing AI coding agents. He is not a software engineer.
